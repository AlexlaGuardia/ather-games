Wilds paths: borders you go round, not across

The real Wilds, drawn from the game's own zone map: 963 squares of 200 m across 67 one-kilometre tiles. Today you can walk straight from Spirit Meadows into the Scald. Each option below turns the zone borders into something you cannot cross on foot, and leaves one way through between each pair of neighbouring zones, placed on the side nearest the Glade. Magii ruled the voids (every one needs a way round or across; a fall costs time, nothing worse).

A · Ridges and passes

Every zone border rises into a ridge too steep to climb. One pass cuts through between each pair of zones.

  • Reads as land, no new fall rules
  • Ridges block the view, so a new zone is a reveal at the pass
  • Lots of ridge to shape (borders are long)

B · Cloud voids and crossings

Every border opens onto the deep. One crossing per pair: a cooled neck of ground or a bridge.

  • You see the next zone across the gap, a pull to go there
  • Falls are real (time and progress lost)
  • Turns the Wilds into islands; strongest Ather look

C · Ridges inside, voids at the edge

my pick

The gentle zones (meadows, thicket, springs, trails) are split by ridges and passes. The hard zones (the Scald, the caverns) sit behind cloud voids with a single crossing each.

  • Danger reads at a glance: a void means a harder place
  • Falls only happen where you chose to go somewhere hard
  • Half the void work of B

How the order falls out

The zones already climb by what grows there. Passes and crossings sit nearest the Glade, so the first one you find leads to the next tier.
