The same in all three (canon, not a choice):

  • Every courser shows the Mana-Carriage's three parts: the mana core (its glow brightens as you push it), the channel trim (a rune-lit line; light runs along it faster with speed) and the driver's rune at the wheel or bars.
  • Kinds are archetypes (coupe, pickup, off-roader, bike), never a real make's design.
  • Polish here is readability and motion: shape and moving light, not small detail.

The pick sets one look per kind; each make's models are then that kind in their own paint and proportions. Today's box blockouts stay until you pick.
