Moonwell Glade, reworked

Today the Glade is a street corner: a 3.2 m pool, Greg's cottage and five Moglins in a ring who all say the same thing. Three layouts below give Greg a real pond beside his home and each of the five a lot with their own station, so a walk through the Glade teaches every trade. Pick one; the maps are to scale, 1 grid square = 5 m.

A · Pondside lane

One lane from the waymark to Greg's door. The tour walks it in order and ends at the kitchen and Greg.

  • Clear story order: shop first, Greg last
  • Long walk on every Wilds trip (~70 m)
  • Lots face the lane, backs to the meadow

B · The green

my pick

The five stand in a horseshoe round a green, with the kitchen at its head and the pond and Greg's home on the open side.

  • Every lot is in view from the green: one camera turn shows the whole Glade on a phone
  • Short trip through (~25 m from waymark to the Wilds)
  • Kitchen at the head reads as the household (canon: where the five gather)

C · Terraces to the water

The Glade steps down a slope. You arrive at the top and walk down past the lots to Greg's home on the water.

  • Best view on arrival: the whole Glade and pond below you
  • Needs a slope in the terrain (new ground shaping)
  • Zigzag is slow on a phone

Your calls

  1. Layout A, B or C (or move lots on the one you like).
  2. Pond size: these are 40 to 80 m across, about 13 to 25 times today's pool. Bigger is fine; it also becomes the Glade's fishing water.
  3. Tour order. In B, Greg walks you round the green clockwise from the kitchen; in A and C the path sets the order.
