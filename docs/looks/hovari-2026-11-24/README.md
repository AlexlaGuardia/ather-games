(3) The skinned ground keep in spirit-rig-body needs no change (it only lifts what is under 0, it never fires); the un-rigged groundLift, pitch-to-slope and any foot or box check must be off for a hoverer. (4) Height over slopes and stairs: root y keeps coming from the ground under it; ease it (about 0.25 s, never under ground + 0.30 m) so a step is a glide, and drop `b_hover` under low ceilings.
