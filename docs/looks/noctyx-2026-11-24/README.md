- Sculpted in code (tools/sculpt.py on the Vulnyx v2 helpers), no outside service. Closed, one island, 0 handles: 5,776 body + 1,430 unlit tris (eyes, lids, brows, nose, mouth, claws).
- Wings: one thin closed sheet a side (8 mm at game size) with an arm + three two-joint finger bones (8 bones a wing). `fold()` in rig.py lays every bone on an ellipse round the body, so the sheet bends round it as a cloak and meets on the chest; flying, the same bones draw it out about 1.4x (span 0.60 m).
- Clips under the game's names: idle (cloak, ear twitch, cloak shift), walk = two-footed hop, run = FLY (lowest point 0.17 m up), sit, lie = sleep (cloak up to the nose, lids down); plus glide and hang (no game clips yet).

Head down, she clears her own ground by 17 mm.
- Numbers for wiring: h 0.45, glbLen about 0.50 (0.228 m deep in idle), clipV walk 0.55/0.45, run 2.8/0.45, runAt about [0.9, 0.75], hull 0.024, neck b_neck, head b_head. Look used: emissive violet (0.60, 0.52, 0.94) at 0.10, hull (0.72, 0.64, 1) at 0.38.
- Dark on dark: on a near-black ground with the lit colour cut to a third she still reads at 4.8 m on a phone (pale hull edge, pale membrane, unlit eyes). She is the darkest of the four; keep the violet floor and hull when wiring.
