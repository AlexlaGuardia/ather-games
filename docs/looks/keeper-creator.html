Keeper creator

A rough creator on the cartoon Alkin rig. Frame is the one choice that plays: it sets the PvP hitbox. Everything else is look-only. Drag the keeper to turn them; Random rolls a whole keeper.

Floating limbs: no arms or legs, the hands and feet hover where they would be. It is a body style, and it would be a canon call for Magii if it means Alkin look like that. Not in this mock: names, voices, species traits, glowing colours.
