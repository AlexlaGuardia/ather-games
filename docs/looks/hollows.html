Three bodies for the Hollows, each shown in all three densities: warden (most gathered), stalker, and the floating caster. Same grey goop in every option; what differs is the build and how the attack is telegraphed. Drag a card to turn it. Play the actions to compare the tell, the hit and the death.

Held for every option (from the brief, not a choice):

  • No light of its own. Today's game Hollow glows faintly grey (an emissive), which the brief bars; all three options drop it. The shine you see is the ground's own colour reflected, so a Hollow on a tended plot looks wet and gold, and one in a greyfield looks dry and hard to see. Switch the ground to compare.
  • One substance gathered on a seed: a denser core, the edges always shedding drips that fall and are drawn back.
  • No face, no kit. The head only tilts.
  • The death is cohesion lost: it sags flat, then the ground's own colour blooms up from the floor where it stood.
  • Hit: the body is knocked back and a lump flies off and crawls home. The board also shows a brief white flash; the brief bars a Hollow owning light, so the flash is your call (it can go and the knock-back alone carries the hit).

At 30 m (the View switch) the camera really stands 30 m off with the game's lens, so the figures are as small as a phone shows them; that is the size the silhouette has to work at. The option you pick replaces the game's blob body; today's body stays live until then.
