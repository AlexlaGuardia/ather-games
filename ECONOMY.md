## What canon already gives us
- **The Ather Trading Co.** is Benji's (the merchant). **Lingston** is its face and a public gate-landing.
- The spine is **Ather Trading Co. → Grand Exchange** (the GE at the Citadel: the player market).
- **One coin: Marks** (Mark → Crown → Sovereign; 1 / 10 / 100).
- **The Ather has no ore.** Everything there is grown or condensed. **Manalic crystal** is its answer to metal.
  **Metal in the Ather is evidence** of the Citadel.
- **Goods cross the gates; coursers don't** (mana-tech stays at the gate). The Co. **never trades spirits**
  (that is the cage-market).

## The loop in one line
**Walk the Ather to gather what grows → drive Athernyx to gather, haul and salvage → trade across the gates →
build your courser (and your keeper) from both.**

So the top of each ladder pulls from **the other world**: the drive-side player needs Ather crystal for a Prism

## How goods move
- **Gates move goods, not vehicles.** You drive cargo to a landing (Rune Hold square, Lingston) and it crosses there.
- **Carry capacity is a courser stat.** The Darter carries a little; the **Hauler** carries a lot. That is the
  Hauler's reason to exist.
- **Distance and freshness:** sea Rinn spoils on a clock, so a coast-to-market run is a race. Timber is heavy, so
  it wants a Hauler.

## Two markets
1. **The Co.'s contract board** (Rune Hold, Lingston, later the traveling trader): *"haul 20 timber to Millbrook"*.
   Pays Marks + **deeds**; deeds unlock parts (the canon "earn" lane). The Co. sets **base prices** so the world
   has an economy before many players do.
2. **The Grand Exchange** (Citadel): player buy/sell orders. **Later**, once there are players to trade with.

## Buy / find / earn (canon lanes, Jin placement)
- **Buy:** Rune Hold + town craft-shops sell Cell / Etched / Cut parts and materials.
- **Find:** salvage sites on the continent drop archaic parts (a found Lattice Core is a good day).
- **Earn:** Co. contracts → deeds → the higher tiers.

## Start small (v1)
One coin (Marks), ~6 goods a side, one contract board at Rune Hold, buy + earn for parts, cargo on the Darter.
Then: the Hauler, Lingston's board, salvage finds, perishables, the GE.
